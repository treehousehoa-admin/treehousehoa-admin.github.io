About the Association

The Treehouse Homeowners Association, Inc. is the homeowners association responsible for the administration and common-area affairs of a residential condominium community at 906 N Kings Rd in West Hollywood, California.

The Association exists to serve the condominium community and its members, including coordinating common-area maintenance, Association administration, and communications with homeowners, residents, and service providers. The Association does not solicit customers or engage in marketing or promotional activities.
