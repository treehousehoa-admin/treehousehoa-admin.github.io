SMS Opt-In

You will receive SMS messages from The Treehouse Homeowners Association, Inc. only after you have opted in to receive them.

You may opt in by providing your mobile phone number directly to The Treehouse Homeowners Association, Inc. and affirmatively agreeing to receive SMS text messages, or by initiating an SMS conversation with the Association at (323) 906-7227.

By opting in, you consent to receive recurring, non-marketing, Association-related SMS messages from The Treehouse Homeowners Association, Inc. at the mobile phone number you provide.

Consent to receive SMS messages is voluntary and is not a condition of any purchase, payment, or other transaction with the Association.

The Treehouse Homeowners Association, Inc. Privacy Policy for Messaging

At The Treehouse Homeowners Association, Inc. (the “Association,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), we respect your privacy. By opting into our SMS messaging service, you agree to the following terms regarding how we handle your data.

Data Collection

We collect or maintain your name, email address, and mobile phone number when you sign up for SMS communications.

This information is gathered when you provide your contact information directly to the Association and affirmatively agree to receive SMS messages, or when you initiate an SMS conversation with the Association.

Data Usage

We use your data solely for informational, non-marketing Association-related communications.

These communications may include maintenance and repair coordination, scheduling, property access, Association administration, notices, service-provider coordination, and responses to Association-related inquiries.

The Association does not use SMS messaging for advertising, sales, or promotional marketing.

Data Security

We protect your data using reasonable storage and access-control measures designed to prevent unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

Data Retention

We retain your SMS subscription and contact information for as long as you are subscribed to our SMS messaging service and as reasonably necessary to document consent and messaging preferences.

You may request deletion from our active SMS messaging records at any time. Certain Association records may be retained where required by law or legitimate Association recordkeeping obligations.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply. Your mobile carrier may charge fees for sending or receiving text messages, especially if you do not have an unlimited texting or data plan.

Messages are recurring and message frequency varies based on Association and communication needs.

Help and Cancellation

Contact The Treehouse Homeowners Association, Inc. at (323) 906-7227 or contact@treehousehoa.org for HELP or to STOP or CANCEL receiving messages.

Opt-Out

You can opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP or CANCEL to any SMS message from the Association.

You may also request to stop receiving SMS messages by emailing contact@treehousehoa.org or contacting the Association at (323) 906-7227.

After unsubscribing, you will receive a final SMS confirming that you have unsubscribed, and we will remove your number from our active SMS messaging list within 24 hours.

You can send HELP for additional assistance and will receive information including our phone number, email address, and website. We are here to help you.

Carrier Non-Liability

Carriers are not liable for any delayed or undelivered messages.

Non-Sharing Clauses

The Treehouse Homeowners Association, Inc. does not share your data with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

The Treehouse Homeowners Association, Inc. does not sell, rent, or share collected mobile phone numbers, mobile opt-in information, or SMS consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Mobile opt-in information and SMS consent are used only for the Association's own communications and are not transferred to another organization for its own marketing or promotional use.

The Association may use service providers solely as necessary to provide communications and other Association services. Those service providers are not authorized to use mobile opt-in information or SMS consent for their own marketing or promotional purposes.
