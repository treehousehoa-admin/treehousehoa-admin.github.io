Privacy Policy & SMS Terms and Conditions

The Treehouse Homeowners Association, Inc.

Effective September 24, 2026

Privacy Policy

The Treehouse Homeowners Association, Inc. (the “Association,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects the privacy of homeowners, residents, vendors, service providers, and others who communicate with the Association. This policy describes how we handle personal information in connection with Association administration and communications, including SMS text messaging.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with the Association, we may receive information such as:

  • Your name and contact information, including email address and phone number.
  • Your relationship to the Association, such as homeowner, resident, vendor, or service provider.
  • Association-related communications and information you voluntarily provide to us.
  • Your consent or preferences regarding SMS communications.

How We Use Information

We use personal information only for legitimate Association purposes, including to:

  • Administer the homeowners association and condominium community.
  • Communicate with homeowners, residents, vendors, and service providers.
  • Coordinate maintenance, repairs, access, scheduling, and other Association matters.
  • Respond to questions, requests, and other communications.
  • Send SMS messages when the recipient has consented to receive them.
  • Meet legal, accounting, recordkeeping, and other Association obligations.

No Marketing or Sale of Personal Information

The Association does not engage in marketing or promotional messaging and does not sell or rent personal information.

We do not share, sell, rent, or provide mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in data, or messaging consent to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

We may use service providers that process information on our behalf to support Association operations or communications, or disclose information when required by law. Such use does not authorize those parties to use mobile opt-in information for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

Website Privacy

This website is informational only. It does not contain advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, or contact forms, and it does not intentionally set tracking cookies.

If you contact us using an email or telephone link, the information you provide is handled as described in this policy.

Data Retention and Security

We retain Association-related information for as long as reasonably necessary for Association administration, recordkeeping, legal obligations, and the purposes described in this policy.

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures appropriate to the nature of the information we maintain.

SMS Messaging Terms & Conditions

These SMS Messaging Terms & Conditions apply to text messages sent by or on behalf of The Treehouse Homeowners Association, Inc.

By providing your mobile phone number and affirmatively opting in to receive SMS communications from the Association, you agree to these SMS Messaging Terms & Conditions and consent to receive SMS text messages from the Association at the mobile number you provide.

Where you initiate an SMS conversation with the Association, your initiation of that conversation may also serve as consent to receive conversational, Association-related responses by SMS.

Purpose of the SMS Messaging Program

The Association uses SMS only for non-marketing, Association-related communications with homeowners, residents, vendors, and service providers who have consented to receive messages.

Messages may concern maintenance and repair coordination, scheduling, property access, Association administration, notices, service-provider coordination, or responses to Association-related inquiries.

The Association does not use SMS for advertising, sales, or promotional marketing.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on Association and communication needs. The number of messages you receive will depend on the nature and duration of the Association-related matter or conversation.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply according to the terms of your wireless service plan. The Association does not charge a separate fee for SMS communications.

Consent

Consent to receive SMS messages is voluntary and is not a condition of any purchase, payment, or other transaction with the Association.

You may decline SMS communications and communicate with the Association by other available means, including email at contact@treehousehoa.org .

Opting Out

You may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to any SMS message from the Association.

After you opt out, you may receive one confirmation message acknowledging your request. You will receive no further SMS messages from the Association unless you later opt in again.

Help

Reply HELP for assistance or contact the Association at contact@treehousehoa.org .

Delivery

Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Availability and delivery of SMS messages may depend on your wireless carrier, device, and network availability.

SMS Privacy

Mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in data, and messaging consent are not shared, sold, rented, or provided to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Information associated with SMS communications is handled in accordance with the Privacy Policy above.
